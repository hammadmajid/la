\subsection*{Solution}

\[
	D = \begin{vmatrix}
		2 & 3 \\
		4 & -1
	\end{vmatrix} = 2(-1) - 3(4) = -14
\]

\[
	D_x = \begin{vmatrix}
		7 & 3 \\
		1 & -1
	\end{vmatrix} = 7(-1) - 3(1) = -10
\]

\[
	D_y = \begin{vmatrix}
		2 & 7 \\
		4 & 1
	\end{vmatrix} = 2(1) - 7(4) = -26
\]

\begin{align*}
	x &= \frac{D_x}{D} = \frac{-10}{-14} = \frac{5}{7} \\
	y &= \frac{D_y}{D} = \frac{-26}{-14} = \frac{13}{7}
\end{align*}

Therefore: $x = \frac{5}{7}, y = \frac{13}{7}$
