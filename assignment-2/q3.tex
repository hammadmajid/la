\subsection*{Solution}

Starting with:
\[
	C = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		2 & 4 & 7 \\
		3 & 6 & 10
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		3 & 6 & 10
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{21} = 2
\]

Zero pivot at position $(2,2)$. The matrix does not have a standard LU factorization without row permutations.

With pivoting, swap $R_2$ and $R_3$:
\[
	PC = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		3 & 6 & 10 \\
		2 & 4 & 7
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 3R_1$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		2 & 4 & 7
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{21} = 3
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - 2R_1$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		0 & 0 & 1
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{31} = 2
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - R_2$:
\[
	U = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		0 & 0 & 0
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{32} = 1
\]

Therefore:
\[
	P = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 0 & 0 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		0 & 1 & 0
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad L = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 0 & 0 \\
		3 & 1 & 0 \\
		2 & 1 & 1
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad U = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 3 \\
		0 & 0 & 1 \\
		0 & 0 & 0
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

The matrix $C$ does not have a standard LU factorization without pivoting.
