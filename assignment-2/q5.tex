\subsection*{Solution}

\textbf{Part 1: Find $T(3,-2,5)$}

The standard matrix of $T$ is formed by the images of the standard basis vectors as columns:
\[
	A = \begin{bmatrix}
		T(e_1) & T(e_2) & T(e_3)
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		2 & -1 & 4 \\
		1 & 3 & -2
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

To find $T(3,-2,5)$, we multiply:
\begin{align*}
	T(3,-2,5) &= A\begin{bmatrix}
		3 \\ -2 \\ 5
	\end{bmatrix} \\
	&= \begin{bmatrix}
		2 & -1 & 4 \\
		1 & 3 & -2
	\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
		3 \\ -2 \\ 5
	\end{bmatrix} \\
	&= \begin{bmatrix}
		2(3) + (-1)(-2) + 4(5) \\
		1(3) + 3(-2) + (-2)(5)
	\end{bmatrix} \\
	&= \begin{bmatrix}
		6 + 2 + 20 \\
		3 - 6 - 10
	\end{bmatrix} \\
	&= \begin{bmatrix}
		28 \\ -13
	\end{bmatrix}
\end{align*}

\[
	\boxed{T(3,-2,5) = (28, -13)}
\]

\textbf{Part 2: Determine the kernel of T}

The kernel of $T$ consists of all vectors $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{3}$ such that $T(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{0}$.

We need to solve $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		2 & -1 & 4 \\
		1 & 3 & -2
	\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
		x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		0 \\ 0
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

Set up the augmented matrix:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		2 & -1 & 4 & 0 \\
		1 & 3 & -2 & 0
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 3 & -2 & 0 \\
		2 & -1 & 4 & 0
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 3 & -2 & 0 \\
		0 & -7 & 8 & 0
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow -\frac{1}{7}R_2$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 3 & -2 & 0 \\
		0 & 1 & -\frac{8}{7} & 0
	\end{array}\right)
\]

From the second row: $x_2 = \frac{8}{7}x_3$

From the first row: $x_1 + 3x_2 - 2x_3 = 0 \Rightarrow x_1 = 2x_3 - 3 \cdot \frac{8}{7}x_3 = 2x_3 - \frac{24}{7}x_3 = -\frac{10}{7}x_3$

Let $x_3 = t$ (free variable):
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		-\frac{10}{7}t \\ \frac{8}{7}t \\ t
	\end{bmatrix} = t\begin{bmatrix}
		-\frac{10}{7} \\ \frac{8}{7} \\ 1
	\end{bmatrix} = \frac{t}{7}\begin{bmatrix}
		-10 \\ 8 \\ 7
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

\[
	\boxed{\text{Ker}(T) = \text{span}\left\{\begin{bmatrix}
		-10 \\ 8 \\ 7
	\end{bmatrix}\right\}}
\]
