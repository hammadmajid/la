\subsection*{Solution}

\[
	D = \begin{vmatrix}
		1 & 2 & 1 \\
		2 & 1 & 1 \\
		1 & 1 & k
	\end{vmatrix}
\]
\begin{align*}
	D &= 1\begin{vmatrix}
		1 & 1 \\
		1 & k
	\end{vmatrix} - 2\begin{vmatrix}
		2 & 1 \\
		1 & k
	\end{vmatrix} + 1\begin{vmatrix}
		2 & 1 \\
		1 & 1
	\end{vmatrix} \\
	&= k - 1 - 4k + 2 + 1 = -3k + 2
\end{align*}

For unique solution: $D \neq 0$
\[
	-3k + 2 \neq 0 \Rightarrow k \neq \frac{2}{3}
\]

Therefore: The system has a unique solution for all $k \neq \frac{2}{3}$

When $k = \frac{2}{3}$, check by row reduction:

\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\
		2 & 1 & 1 & 2 \\
		1 & 1 & \frac{2}{3} & 3
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\
		0 & -3 & -1 & 0 \\
		1 & 1 & \frac{2}{3} & 3
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - R_1$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\
		0 & -3 & -1 & 0 \\
		0 & -1 & -\frac{1}{3} & 2
	\end{array}\right)
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - \frac{1}{3}R_2$:
\[
	\left(\begin{array}{ccc|c}
		1 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\
		0 & -3 & -1 & 0 \\
		0 & 0 & 0 & 2
	\end{array}\right)
\]

Last row gives $0 = 2$, contradiction. When $k = \frac{2}{3}$, the system has no solution.
