\subsection*{Solution}

Apply $T$ to standard basis vectors:
\begin{align*}
	T(1,0) &= (2, 1) \\
	T(0,1) &= (3, -4)
\end{align*}

Standard matrix:
\[
	A = \begin{bmatrix}
		2 & 3 \\
		1 & -4
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

Calculate determinant:
\[
	\det(A) = \begin{vmatrix}
		2 & 3 \\
		1 & -4
	\end{vmatrix} = 2(-4) - 3(1) = -8 - 3 = -11
\]

Since $\det(A) = -11 \neq 0$, the transformation is invertible.

Inverse matrix:
\[
	A^{-1} = \frac{1}{-11}\begin{bmatrix}
		-4 & -3 \\
		-1 & 2
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		\frac{4}{11} & \frac{3}{11} \\
		\frac{1}{11} & -\frac{2}{11}
	\end{bmatrix}
\]
