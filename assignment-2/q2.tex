\subsection*{Solution}

\textbf{Part 1: Find LU decomposition}

Starting with:
\[
	B = \begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		8 & 5 & 3 \\
		12 & 7 & 11
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

$R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		0 & 3 & -1 \\
		12 & 7 & 11
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{21} = 2
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - 3R_1$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		0 & 3 & -1 \\
		0 & 4 & 5
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{31} = 3
\]

$R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - \frac{4}{3}R_2$:
\[
	U = \begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		0 & 3 & -1 \\
		0 & 0 & \frac{19}{3}
	\end{bmatrix}, \quad l_{32} = \frac{4}{3}
\]

\[
	L = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 0 & 0 \\
		2 & 1 & 0 \\
		3 & \frac{4}{3} & 1
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

\[
	\boxed{B = LU = \begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 0 & 0 \\
		2 & 1 & 0 \\
		3 & \frac{4}{3} & 1
	\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		0 & 3 & -1 \\
		0 & 0 & \frac{19}{3}
	\end{bmatrix}}
\]

\textbf{Part 2: Solve $Bx = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 13 \\ 25 \end{bmatrix}$}

Using $LUx = b$, let $Ux = y$. First solve $Ly = b$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		1 & 0 & 0 \\
		2 & 1 & 0 \\
		3 & \frac{4}{3} & 1
	\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
		y_1 \\ y_2 \\ y_3
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		5 \\ 13 \\ 25
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

Forward substitution:
\begin{align*}
	y_1 &= 5 \\
	2y_1 + y_2 &= 13 \Rightarrow y_2 = 13 - 10 = 3 \\
	3y_1 + \frac{4}{3}y_2 + y_3 &= 25 \Rightarrow y_3 = 25 - 15 - 4 = 6
\end{align*}

Now solve $Ux = y$:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		4 & 1 & 2 \\
		0 & 3 & -1 \\
		0 & 0 & \frac{19}{3}
	\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
		x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3
	\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
		5 \\ 3 \\ 6
	\end{bmatrix}
\]

Back substitution:
\begin{align*}
	\frac{19}{3}x_3 &= 6 \Rightarrow x_3 = \frac{18}{19} \\
	3x_2 - x_3 &= 3 \Rightarrow x_2 = \frac{3 + \frac{18}{19}}{3} = \frac{75}{57} = \frac{25}{19} \\
	4x_1 + x_2 + 2x_3 &= 5 \Rightarrow x_1 = \frac{5 - \frac{25}{19} - \frac{36}{19}}{4} = \frac{\frac{95-61}{19}}{4} = \frac{34}{76} = \frac{17}{38}
\end{align*}

\[
	\boxed{x = \begin{bmatrix}
		\frac{17}{38} \\ \frac{25}{19} \\ \frac{18}{19}
	\end{bmatrix}}
\]
